Planning only. P28's two live integer fragments were slower. P29/P30 instead reduce exact preprocessing cost but still leave a large corrected-consumer cost. A different consumer control can preserve every quantized operand and all eight integer dots: conditionally accumulate all fourK128slabs into one INT32 fragment when their existing restoration exponents are equal. This does not widen quantization scales as M9 does, or assume that typical inputs satisfy the predicate. Actual eligible frequencies, compiled scheduling and performance remain unmeasured.

Each CTA already holds metadata for5correction groups*4Kslabs in the first20lanes of each consumer warp. For the current group, broadcast all four e0 and all four e1 sums. Require e0(K0)=...=e0(K3), e1(K0)=...=e1(K3), and both exponents in[-126,103]. These values are uniform across the consumer warpgroup, while e0/e1 may differ between the two B output halves. Otherwise execute the unchanged P30 per-K restoration path. Do not average, coarsen or replace any exponent, and do not use a predicate based only on one lane's own metadata.

Quantizers clamp each INT8 value to[-127,127]. A pairedK128dot is bounded by2*128*127²=4,129,024. Across four slabs, every integer partial sum has magnitude at most2*512*127²=16,516,096<2²⁴ and far below INT32 overflow. A single-dot group has a tighter bound. Thus every intermediate integer and combined integer converts exactly toFP32. For a common normal power-of-two scale2^e with e in[-126,103], each scaled value and every scaled intermediate sum is exactly representable and finite. The original four RN multiplies/adds from zero and one multiply after integer summation therefore agree, including cancellation. The conservative upper exponent avoids overflow; the lower exponent avoids subnormal rounding. Different Kscales or exceptional exponents must fall back.

The accompanying CPU arithmetic control checks generated bounded signed integer tuples, cancellation boundaries and all230exponents in the permitted interval, comparing FP32 bit patterns for sequential and combined restoration. A differing-scale negative control explicitly disagrees. This tests only restoration arithmetic, not matrix accuracy, metadata selection, WGMMA, synchronization or latency. It does not replace original-input FP64 gates or fresh stress cases.
