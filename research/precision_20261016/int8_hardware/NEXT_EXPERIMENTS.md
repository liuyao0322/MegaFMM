## Two integer accumulator fragments, unchanged K128 scales

A controlled next consumer can use two distinct INT32 fragments, issueK0 thenK1, wait1 and consume only completedK0 whileK1 remains pending. Use constant unrolled K indices or templated references, so indexing the two fragments cannot create dynamic local arrays. Initialize/fence both fragments before the pipeline. Keep the official CuTe accumulator-fence hints; do not remove them to silence a warning. No CPU writes to an in-flight integer fragment. This is an attempt to hide conversions and memory waits, not a claim of fewer integer MMAs or fewer source-level wait instructions.
