The proposed next change is shared/shared WGMMA overlap across two existing operand banks. Keep the same 384 threads, RN FP32 input DAG, one final original-Element cast, seven complete FP32 P accumulators, ascending K16 order, fixed U and epilogue. Read-state and release-state must be separate: wait raw stage s, construct S/T in that bank, execute writer proxy fences and the256-consumer barrier, issue both K16 atoms for each assigned whole P, and commit one group. After stage s>=1, wait_group1 completes group s-1, then release only bank s-1. At s=0 there is no prior bank to release. After the final commit, wait_group0 precedes releasing the last bank. Both producer-tail bank waits and CTA drain must finish before P/U publication.

The producer may load stage s+2 only after BOTH complete consumer groups release stage s. Transforming stage s+1 may overlap stage s WGMMA because the operands occupy the other bank. Every transform and descriptor instruction must use registers outside all pending P ranges. Same-shape WGMMA accesses to the same accumulators have the ISA ordering exception; generic P reads/writes and premature bank reuse remain forbidden. The consumer barrier orders each stage's final shared writes; it does not itself wait for outstanding WGMMA. These are design inferences from the [CUDA12.4.1 PTX WGMMA rules](https://docs.nvidia.com/cuda/archive/12.4.1/parallel-thread-execution/index.html#asynchronous-warpgroup-level-matrix-instructions-wgmma-fence) and [wait_group rules](https://docs.nvidia.com/cuda/archive/12.4.1/parallel-thread-execution/index.html#asynchronous-warpgroup-level-matrix-instructions-wgmma-wait-group). They are not compiled safety proof.

The pinned [CUTLASS3.5.1 PipelineAsync](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/NVIDIA/cutlass/v3.5.1/include/cutlass/pipeline/sm90_pipeline.hpp) keeps independent full/empty barriers for two banks. Its release state selects a bank while wait uses bank and phase; producer_tail acquires both banks. A future implementation needs separate read and delayed-release states with exactly one256-thread release per stage. Producer start phase1 and consumer phase0, plus raw_done opposite producer phase, remain the candidate conventions. The model annotates these generation-derived phase formulas through repeated bank reuse. It does not emulate or validate physical mbarrier state transitions, CuTe layouts, TMA hardware or actual thread instructions.
